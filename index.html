HOW TO PLAY

  1. Tap or drag across rock to mark tunnels, starting from the shaft or the surface. Tap a mark again to clear it.
  2. Your robots dig the marks, fill their hoppers with ore, then haul it up the shaft and sell it. Plain rock sells as scrap for a little.
  3. Every find rolls a rarity. Deeper rock rolls better.
  4. ROBOTS upgrades one machine (and transforms it). BASE upgrades everything.
  5. Look around: PAN tool, two fingers, right-drag, or the mouse wheel.
  6. Zoom for a closer look: pinch, +/− buttons or keys, or ctrl + wheel.
